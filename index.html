The Loop

STEP 01

Crack the set

Everyone opens the same 18 packs of the newest set in the sim — ideally live together. The pack-opening is half the night.

STEP 02

Grow your pool

Add every card you pulled to your personal cumulative collection. It only ever grows — nothing is spent.

STEP 03

Rebuild the deck

Construct a fresh 60-card deck from your entire pool, not just the new set. Full rebuilds allowed every session.

STEP 04

Duel

Run the night's Swiss rounds in TTS. Log results to the season standings, then queue up the next set.

02

The Golden Rule

Read this one first

Basic Energy is free. Special Energy is pulled.

A 60-card deck needs 12–20 Energy. If you could only run the Energy you opened, decks would be unplayable for the first month. So basic Energy sits outside the pool entirely — and in TTS you just spawn it from the card database. Everything else you play must come from cards you actually pulled.

// UNLIMITED
Basic EnergyGrass, Fire, Water, Lightning, Psychic, Fighting, Metal, Darkness. Add as many as you like.
// PULL IT
Special EnergyDouble Colorless, Rainbow, and any other named Energy count toward your pool like any card.

03

Deckbuilding

  • Deck sizeExactly 60 cards. Free basic Energy conveniently fills the gap when your early pool is thin.
  • Copy limitMax 4 of any card (basic Energy exempt). You run only the copies you actually pulled.
  • BasicsAt least one Basic Pokémon — realistically several. Thin Basic counts mean brutal mulligans, which is a real early-game tension.
  • RebuildFull teardown allowed every session from your whole cumulative pool. Chase a new archetype whenever a set unlocks it.
  • BansStart with none. A limited pool can't easily assemble the degenerate Unlimited combos. The Commissioner adds restrictions only if something dominates.
04

Battle Rules

  • As printedPlay cards by their era's text — in Base–Neo, Weakness doubles damage (×2, not +30) and Trainers have no once-per-turn limit.
  • Turn 1House rule for fairness: the player going first doesn't draw and can't attack on their first turn.
  • Win byTake all 6 Prizes, leave the opponent with no Pokémon in play, or deck them out at the start of their turn.
  • MatchBest of one per Swiss pairing to keep nights moving. Bump to Bo3 for the era-championship finale.

06

The Season

Swiss rounds, points that carry

With a full table, a round-robin is too many games. Each night, run 3 Swiss rounds — pair players by current record so games stay competitive. Points accumulate across the entire series into a running leaderboard.

Miss a night? No problem — Swiss + cumulative points means the league survives uneven attendance. Latecomers just start further down the board.

Era Championships

At the close of each era on the Season Track — Wizards, Neo, e-Card — the top two on points play a Bo3 final for that era's title. Reset nothing; points roll onward. Win the most era titles and you take the overall crown when the series ends.

07

Tabletop Simulator Setup

  1. In the sim, select the newest set, set quantity to 18, and Draft or Auto Open. Then hit Export → TTS.
  2. In Tabletop Simulator, load a maintained Pokémon TCG mod — Pepperoni's table is the actively-updated one with the full old-card database and a decklist-import tablet.
  3. Keep one saved object as your personal collection. Each session, add the packs you just exported to it — that's your growing pool.
  4. Build your 60-card deck from your collection, spawning unlimited basic Energy straight from the mod's database.
  5. Sit in your player color, set up, and duel. Save your deck as an object so next session you can tweak instead of rebuild from scratch.

Lighter alternatives if TTS is fussy for someone: the sim's own on-site Play table, or the browser-based ptcgsim.online. But TTS gives the fullest control over these old cards.

08

Optional Spice

🔁 Trading Window

Open a trade phase between sessions. Cross-collection deals add a negotiation layer the source material never had — and help everyone's decks come online.

🏆 Pull of the Night

Each era has a white whale: 1st-edition holos, Neo Destiny Shinings, e-Card Crystals. Vote a best-pull each session for pure bragging rights.

⚖️ Rubber-Band Handicap

Optional constraint nights for whoever's running away with the season — mono-type only, or "no rares." Keeps the back of the pack in it.

📋 The Commissioner

Appoint one person to arbitrate rules, hold the (initially empty) banlist, and keep the standings sheet. Rotate the role by era if you like.
