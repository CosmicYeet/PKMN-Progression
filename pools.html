Card Pools

The single source of truth for what everyone owns. Open a trainer to see their whole collection — every card they've pulled across all sessions, with a running copy count. If a card isn't here, it can't be in their deck.

How to log your pulls

  1. Right after opening, click Share your Pulls → Copy Link in the sim — a permanent snapshot of that session's pulls.
  2. Drop the link where your group collects them (a Discord channel works well), tagged with your name and the set.
  3. The Commissioner pastes each link into the league Google Sheet — the player's row, the column for that set. This page reads it live and renders the cards.

Cards load from the share links when you open a trainer, so the first open takes a moment. Counts are summed across every session that player has logged.
