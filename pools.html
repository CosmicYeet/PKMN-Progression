How to log your pulls

  1. Right after opening, click Share your Pulls → Copy Link in the sim.
  2. Send the link to the Commissioner, tagged with your name and the set you opened.
  3. The Commissioner pastes each link into the league Google Sheet — player's row, the set's column. Card pools and the Deck Builder refresh after the Commissioner manually runs Build card pools and publishes the updated data; spreadsheet edits alone do not update these pages.
