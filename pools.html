Card Pools

The single source of truth for what everyone owns. Every share-link is a frozen snapshot of exactly what a player pulled — decks are audited against these. If a card isn't in your logged pool, it can't be in your deck. Pulled live from the league spreadsheet.

How to log your pulls

  1. Right after opening, click Share your Pulls → Copy Link in the sim. That link is a permanent snapshot of exactly what you pulled that session.
  2. Drop the link where your group collects them (a Discord channel works well), tagged with your name and the set.
  3. The Commissioner pastes each link into the league Google Sheet — the player's row, the column for that set. This page reads the sheet live, so it updates on its own.

You don't need GitHub or spreadsheet access to take part — you just share links, and one person keeps the sheet.
